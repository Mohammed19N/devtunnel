Questions

What is the browser warning page?

On Free, a visitor who opens your tunnel in a browser first sees a page that names the tunnel's owner and asks them to continue. It helps stop phishing on shared domains. Webhooks, API clients and curl are not browsers, so their requests reach your app directly.

What counts as bandwidth?

The bytes of requests and responses that pass through your tunnels in a calendar month (UTC). When you reach the limit, your tunnels stop serving until the next month or until you upgrade.

Can I run Passway without an account?

Yes. Run your own server and none of these limits apply. See the self-hosting guide.
